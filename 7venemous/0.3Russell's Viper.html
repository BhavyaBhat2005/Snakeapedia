🐍 Russell's-Viper 🐍

What is a Russells-Viper?

The Russell's viper (Daboia russelii) is a highly venomous snake found across the Indian subcontinent, known for its distinctive appearance and potent venom. It has a robust body with a yellowish-brown color marked by three rows of dark oval spots outlined in white. Typically growing up to 1.5 meters in length, it inhabits grasslands, farmlands, and areas near human settlements. The snake is nocturnal and feeds primarily on rodents, birds, and lizards, playing an important role in controlling pests. However, it is highly dangerous due to its hemotoxic venom, which can cause severe pain, bleeding, kidney failure, and even death if untreated. Known for its aggressive nature, the Russell's viper hisses loudly
when threatened and strikes quickly, making immediate medical attention and antivenom crucial in case of a bite.

Interesting Facts: Russell's viper venom can cause long-term hormonal imbalances by damaging the pituitary gland.

Habitat and Behavior

The Russell's viper (Daboia russelii) thrives in a variety of habitats, including open grasslands, agricultural fields, scrublands, and coastal lowlands. It is commonly found near human settlements due to the abundance of rodents, its primary prey. However, it avoids dense forests and extremely arid deserts. The snake is primarily nocturnal, hunting at night, but it may bask in the sun during the day. Known for its aggressive behavior, the Russell's viper becomes defensive when threatened, producing a loud hissing sound as a warning before striking. It relies on its excellent camouflage to ambush prey and plays a vital role in controlling rodent populations in agricultural regions.

How to Respond to a Russells-Viper Encounter?

1.Do Not Approach or Provoke
Russell’s vipers are highly defensive and may strike if they feel threatened. Maintain a safe distance.
2.Stay Still or Move Away Slowly
Sudden movements can startle the snake. Back away slowly without making any sudden gestures.
3.Do Not Attempt to Handle or Kill
Handling or trying to kill the snake increases the risk of being bitten. Let it move away on its own.
4.Watch Your Step
If in snake-prone areas, wear protective footwear and be cautious when stepping in tall grass, under rocks, or near burrows.
5. Alert Local Authorities
If the snake is in a residential or high-traffic area, contact wildlife rescue services or local snake catchers for safe removal.
6. Call Local Wildlife Authorities or Animal Control:
Professionals have the proper tools and knowledge to relocate the snake safely.
7. In Case of a Bite
Seek immediate medical attention. Keep the victim calm, immobilize the affected limb, and avoid home remedies like cutting the wound or sucking out venom.
8. ✅ What NOT to Do:
🚫 Don’t attempt to kill the snake—it’s often illegal and unnecessary.
